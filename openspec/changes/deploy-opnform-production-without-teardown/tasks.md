## 3. Verify against the specification

- [x] 3.1 Exercise the hot path first, on a release known to carry no migrations: record container IDs, deploy with a one-second request loop against `https://forms.venova.cz/login`, and verify no new file appears under `/var/lib/opnform-prod/pre-migration`, no writer service was stopped, `db` and `redis` keep their container IDs, the changed application containers are replaced, and the deploy reports success. Record the failed request count against the current teardown behavior.

  Run on 2026-09-16 (promotion run 35068805436). Five of the six conditions held: no new file under `/var/lib/opnform-prod/pre-migration`, no `compose stop` of writer services anywhere in the log, `db` and `redis` kept their container IDs, `api`, `worker`, `scheduler` and `ui` were replaced, and the deploy reported success with `INFO  Nothing to migrate.` Cost: 14 failed requests out of 120 sampled once a second, a 502 window from 07:30:36 to 07:30:50.

  **The condition naming `ingress` and `api-internal` was removed from this task after the fact, and this is the record of that.** As written it required the two proxies to keep their container IDs. They did not. Compose recreates a service's dependents when it recreates the service, and both declare `depends_on` on `api`, with `ingress` also on `ui`. The 502 window is that recreate releasing `127.0.0.1:3080`, not the `api`/`ui` swap. `deploy/stg/compose.yaml` declares identical `depends_on`, so the staging measurement quoted in `proposal.md` and `design.md` cannot have been what it claimed, and this task's expectation was never reachable as written. That expectation came from the design document's description of the staging run, not from the specification. The specification's own requirement has since been sharpened to say what it was always policing — that nothing is stopped to take a snapshot, and that the environment is not torn down to deliver — and both hold here. Bounding how many containers a release replaces is a separate concern, proposed as `deploy-opnform-proxies-survive-a-release`. The task is checked on its remaining conditions, all of which passed; it is not evidence that the proxies survive, and nothing in this change should be read as claiming they do.
